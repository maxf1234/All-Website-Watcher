Nick Paumgarten
Nick Paumgarten has been a staff writer at The New Yorker since 2005. Prior to that, he was an editor of the Talk of the Town. He has reported on a wide range of subjects, including hockey , politics , finance , art , music , food , technology , mountaineering , sports-talk radio , elevators , boxer-bartenders , commuters , and canoes . He is the author of “ The Intangibles: Friendship, Fatherhood, and the Love of the Game ” (November, 2026).
