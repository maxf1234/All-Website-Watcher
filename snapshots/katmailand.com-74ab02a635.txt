Due to a high demand for overnight accommodations at Brooks, we operate a random lottery system for the opportunity to book a room at Brooks Lodge.
The application period for the 2027 Brooks Lodge Lottery is closed . The application period for the 2028 Brooks Lodge Lottery will be from December 1st – December 30th, 2026.
However, day trips to Brooks from Anchorage, King Salmon, or our sister lodges are also available. Contact our reservation team for information on other ways to visit Brooks in 2026 or 2027!
2028 Lottery Rules and Guidelines
Applications will be available online from December 1, 2026 through December 30, 2026, and may be submitted via our website.
Each person may submit only 1(ONE) application. Duplicate applications will be disregarded(the LAST application received will be kept). The name that the application is submitted under MUST be traveling on the reservation. Reservations may not be given or transferred to another person or party.
All applications have equal chance by random drawing.
All entries must be RECEIVED by 5:00 PM Alaska Standard Time on December 30, 2026.
All entrants will be contacted between January 3 – February 10, 2027 with either their date to stay at Brooks Lodge, or with their place on the wait list. Entrants are contacted in order that their number is drawn, so you may be contacted any time between January 3 and February 10, 2027.
Reservations will be made based ONLY on the information received on your application. Tip: The more flexible you are with the dates requested, the higher chance of receiving a reservation!
If you are selected to receive a reservation in the lottery, you will be emailed your reservation information by February 10, 2027 and a 50% deposit based on 2027 rates will be due within 3 days. Final payments are due 90 days prior to your reservation date. Any rate increase for 2028 will be added to final payment . All cancellation policies apply regardless of rate increase.
Once any payment is made, there is no refund unless rooms are resold. Cancellation fees apply. Please view our cancellation policy on our website .
Group Size Limits
Requests for any specific room or room type are not accepted. If someone checks out of one or more of the individual cabins on the date you are arriving, they will be assigned to whomever was drawn first in the lottery. There are no exceptions.
If one or more nights of your stay falls during July or September, THREE nights is the maximum stay allowed. (For example, if your stay is June 29, 30 and July 1, three nights is the maximum stay allowed). This includes non-consecutive and/or multiple trips.
Each party may only win ONE July or September date in the lottery. Duplicate reservations will be cancelled if one or more names are found to be the same on more than one booking, even after any deposit or final payment has been made. You will receive a refund if the room is resold. All cancellation policies apply.
Brooks Lodge Room Policies
4 persons maximum per room. Each room has 2 sets of twin bunk beds.
5 rooms is the maximum allowed for any 1 party/application. Maximum party size is 20 persons.
2 rooms is the MAXIMUM allowed for double occupancy in any party. Parties requesting more than 2 rooms MUST have a MINIMUM of 3 persons per room.
Groups less than 4 / 1 room maximum per group/company
Groups of 4-8 / 2 rooms maximum per group/company
Groups of 9-11 / 3 rooms maximum per group/company
Groups of 12-14 / 4 rooms maximum per group/company
Groups of 15-20 / 5 rooms maximum per group/company
How The Lottery Is Conducted
Once the lottery entry period has closed, any duplicate entries will be deleted (the LAST application received from a person will be kept), then each remaining unique entry will be assigned a number based on the order it was received.
We will conduct a random number drawing using random number generator.
When we come to your number, we will check to see if any date that you have noted on your form is available starting with your first choice followed by any subsequent choices listed.
If none of your dates are available (or if there are fewer rooms left than what you have requested on your form), you will be placed on the wait list in the order that your number was drawn.
Once all peak dates have been filled (approx. June 25 – August 10 and August 25 – closing), we may begin notifying entrants in groups of 50 of the dates which are still available. Those entrants may then call or email to request an available June or August date if they so choose. Otherwise, they will be added to the wait list.
Anyone who does not receive space during their requested time period will be put on a wait list in the order that their number is drawn and notified by February 10, 2027 of their number on the wait list. If space becomes available, we will email you and you will have 24 hours to confirm the reservation. The wait list will be maintained through April 1, 2028.
Any dates / rooms still available once all applications in the lottery have been drawn and processed will be open to the public for regular reservations by phone or email starting February 11, 2027. All late June, July, August and September dates will fill during the lottery drawing, but some early and mid-June dates will still be available.
Reservations will be made based only on the information received on your application. You will not be contacted to find out if you will take fewer nights, fewer rooms, or different dates than what you have submitted.
If your number is drawn and you receive a date that you requested on your lottery form, you are not on the waitlist for any other date and you may not cancel and go back on the waitlist.
Remember, the more flexible you are with the dates, the higher chance of receiving a reservation! Good luck!
