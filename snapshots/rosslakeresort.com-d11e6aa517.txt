Stay at the Resort
In existence since 1952, Ross Lake Resort is nestled in the heart of the North Cascade Mountains and is comprised of fifteen floating cabins and a marina. As visitors to the National Park increase every year, so does the popularity of our floating mountain oasis.
​
With a season that runs from early June - October 31st, we do book up quickly. But don't worry, there are a few ways you can visit the resort in the future. To get a cabin reservation you must join the lottery list the year you are looking to stay.
Getting a reservation to stay at the resort can take flexibility, but there are other ways to visit and enjoy Ross lake. Learn more about Camping on the lake or Day Visits .
Please note: Ross Lake Resort has "gone viral" on many social media platforms in the last few years. As a result, we have many people who sign-up for the lottery but who end up not following through on their interest. We appreciate your patience as this often requires us to work through hundreds of requests in order to fill a single reservation.

Enter Our Accommodation Lottery - Lottery list resets every January 1st
Whenever we have an opening, we pull directly from our lottery submissions. Here are some tips...
Sign up for our accommodation lottery - Resort staff will fill all open reservations from the lottery list and match entries with available accommodations. You must sign up every calendar year. A 60% of our lodging reservations are reservable one year in advance upon checkout, and 40% of our reservation openings are filled via our lottery list from our website. All reservations cancelled throughout the season are also booked from the lottery list. Last-minute availabilities will be filled first-come-first-serve via the Last-Minute Availability Table below.
Be specific - We filter our submissions based on what we have available and what you're looking for giving everyone a fair shot! The more specific you can be, the better. If you have more flexibility in your request, add that information to the comments section on your submission form.
Be persistent - Be diligent in adding your name to the lottery list each year. We also encourage those with flexible schedules to keep an eye on our Availability Table below for last-minute cancellations.
Be mindful - The lottery list is cleared on December 31st of each year. Parties will need to re-submit their interest for the upcoming season beginning January 1st. We will work to fill all open reservations in May of each year and will continue calling off the lottery list throughout our season.

Accommodations
Keep scrolling for cabin pricing, descriptions, and pictures (prices subject to change)
All of our cabins are completely furnished and include:
Full kitchens with standard appliances, bathrooms, electricity, and hot and cold running water
Towels, bedding, tableware, silverware, pots, and pans, etc.
Propane grill (shared by two cabins) with propane provided
Please Note:
Bring your own food as there is no restaurant or store on-site
There is no charge for children under 4
While we love fur babies of all kinds, we do not allow pets to stay overnight in the cabins with guests ​
There are four styles of cabins to choose from: ​ ​ ​
Little Cabin
Our Little Cabins are a bit smaller and cozier than our Modern Cabins. This quaint cabin offers wood stove heat, a full kitchen, bathroom, one queen bed, and one bunk bed. Last but not least, breathtaking views of Colonial and Pyramid Peaks!
Sleeps: 1-4 people
Nightly Rate: $305 for 1-2 people*
each additional person is $25 per night

Our Modern Cabins offer a spacious dining-living room combined, wood stove or electric heat, a full kitchen, and bathroom. Two to three twin beds (depending on what cabin you're in) and one bunk bed.

​ Our Private Bunkhouses are ideal for larger groups. They offer a large combined kitchen-dining-sleeping area, full kitchen, bathroom, and wood stove heat. There are four or five bunk beds (depending on the cabin) and are perfect for parties of six or more.
Sleeps: 1-10 people
Nightly Rate: $440 for 1-6 people*
Peak Cabin
​ Our Peak Cabins are perfect for creating family memories! This spacious two-story cabin offers an open loft that has one queen-size bed and two twin beds. There is one bedroom on the lower level that has one twin bed and one bunk bed. This cabin is complete with a full kitchen, bathroom with a bathtub, large living/dining area with a pullout sofa sleeper, and wood stove heat. And let's not forget the incredible view of Ross Lake Dam and magnificent mountain peaks!
Sleeps: 1-9 people
Nightly Rate: $520 for 1-4 people*

Cancellation Policy for Lottery Accomodation Selections
Reservations granted through the lottery system will require a deposit equal to the first night's stay at the time of booking. ​
Lottery reservations forfeit deposit upon cancellation
We retain a $25.00 processing/booking fee on all issued refunds
